Policy tailwind — RDSS

The Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme is the Government of India's ₹3.03 lakh crore distribution programme, sanctioned 2021, running to FY 2025–26. Its smart-metering component targets ~250 million prepaid smart meters plus feeder and distribution-transformer metering — the exact measurement layer this system consumes.

This colony

Three synthetic societies on a shared feeder — Society A (60 households, the detailed one), Society B (48, an older/smaller transformer, runs hot), Society C (48, a newer better-rated transformer, has headroom). Fully synthetic: archetype behaviour and appliance ratings are hand-authored, not fitted to a named dataset. Nothing here is claimed as real-time.

Each house's sub-meter

Every house's Load now is its own metered reading — the same granularity a real per-flat sub-meter reports, and the same data path GridBrain's decision ledger reads from directly. No discom API involved; this is a bulk HT society's own billing sub-meters.

What GridBrain actually does here

The curtailment lines in the ledger are the real, already-simulated engine decisions (the same transformer-protection logic that runs in the physics), surfaced in plain English as they happen — not a separate fabricated demo layer. The cross-society "headroom" lines are a live computed comparison across the three transformers, framed as a recommendation: this colony does not yet simulate an automated cross-feeder transfer mechanism, and that's stated here rather than implied.

The sibling single-building benchmark

Aethergrid's forecasting method (95th-percentile chance-constrained dispatch) is validated separately on real meter data: Building Data Genome Project 2 — four real buildings, two years, held-out test months, American sites (Tempe, Arizona), openly disclosed. Only the method transfers, not the load shapes. Tariff, bill engine and calibration are checked against a real published Tamil Nadu (TNERC) order, at aethergrid-vert.vercel.app/method — a different, complementary piece of evidence, not this same system.
